Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how GrigoriyArx ("we," "us," or "our") handles information in ANTHAVEN (the "Game"), a mobile ant colony simulation game.

1. Information you provide

The Game currently does not require account registration, a name, an email address, or other personal information to play. If you contact us by email, we receive the information you choose to include in your message and use it to respond to your inquiry.

2. Game progress and local storage

The Game stores colony progress, settings, and related gameplay information locally on your device so you can continue playing. We do not currently operate a developer-controlled server that receives or stores your game saves. Local game data may be lost if you uninstall the Game, clear its data, or change devices. Device or platform backup features, if enabled, are controlled by their respective providers.

3. Technical and diagnostic information

The Game is built with the Unity engine. Unity Engine Diagnostics is enabled in the current project configuration. Depending on the Unity engine, device, build configuration, and applicable settings, technical diagnostic information may be processed by Unity, such as device or operating-system details, application performance information, and error-related data. We do not use this information to identify players personally.

For details about Unity's data practices, see the Unity Privacy Policy.

4. Analytics, advertising, and accounts

As of the date above, the current Game project does not integrate Firebase, Google AdMob, Unity Ads, or an active Unity Analytics service. The Game also does not currently provide in-game account sign-in or developer-operated cloud save synchronization.

If we introduce analytics, advertising (including optional rewarded ads), or cloud-save/sign-in services in a future version, we will update this Privacy Policy to explain the relevant providers, data practices, and choices before releasing that version.

5. How information is used and shared

Local gameplay data is used to operate the Game and retain your progress. Technical diagnostics, where collected by the engine provider, may be used to maintain stability and troubleshoot problems. We do not sell personal information. Service providers may process technical information under their own privacy terms where their technology is used in the Game.

6. Data retention and your choices

You can remove locally stored Game data through your device's app settings or by uninstalling the Game, subject to any operating-system backup behavior. For questions about diagnostic information or a privacy request, contact us using the email address below. We will review requests and direct you to the relevant service provider where necessary.

7. Children's privacy

The Game is not specifically directed to children. We do not knowingly request personal information from children through the Game. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so we can review the concern.

8. International processing

Third-party technology providers, including Unity where applicable, may process technical information in countries other than your own, in accordance with their policies and applicable law.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the Game's features and services change. The revised version will be posted on this page with an updated date.
