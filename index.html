Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how GrigoriyArx ("we," "us," or "our") handles information in ANTHAVEN (the "Game"), a mobile ant colony simulation game.

1. Information you provide

The Game currently does not require account registration, a name, an email address, or other personal information to play. If you contact us by email, we receive the information you choose to include in your message and use it to respond to your inquiry.

2. Game progress and local storage

The Game stores colony progress, settings, and related gameplay information locally on your device so you can continue playing. We do not currently operate a developer-controlled server that receives or stores your game saves. Local game data may be lost if you uninstall the Game, clear its data, or change devices. Device or platform backup features, if enabled, are controlled by their respective providers.

3. Technical and diagnostic information

The Game is built with the Unity engine. Unity Engine Diagnostics is enabled in the current project configuration. Depending on the Unity engine, device, build configuration, and applicable settings, technical diagnostic information may be processed by Unity, such as device or operating-system details, application performance information, and error-related data. We do not use this information to identify players personally.

For details about Unity's data practices, see the Unity Privacy Policy.

4. Analytics

The Game uses Google Analytics for Firebase, provided by Google LLC, to understand how the Game is played and to improve it. Firebase Analytics may collect app-instance identifiers, device and operating-system information, app version, approximate location derived from the IP address, session information, and in-game events such as tutorial progress, colony milestones, feeding, decoration placement, use of game features (for example ant possession, photo mode, and the in-game guide), and similar gameplay statistics.

We do not send your name, email address, or other directly identifying information to Firebase. We use this information to analyze gameplay patterns and improve the Game, not to personally identify players, and we do not use it for advertising.

Your choice. Analytics is optional. You can turn it off at any time in the Game: open Menu → Settings and clear the "Help improve the game" checkbox. While it is off, the Game sends no analytics events and the analytics data stored on your device is reset. You can turn it back on the same way.

Retention. Event-level analytics data is kept for 2 months under our Google Analytics data-retention setting and is then deleted. Aggregated, non-identifying reports (for example, daily player counts) may be kept longer.

For details about how Google processes this data, see How Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Firebase Privacy and Security page.

4a. Advertising and accounts

As of the date above, the Game does not integrate an advertising network, in-game account sign-in, or developer-operated cloud save synchronization. If we introduce advertising (including optional rewarded ads), sign-in, or cloud-save services in a future version, we will update this Privacy Policy before releasing that version.

5. How information is used and shared

Local gameplay data is used to operate the Game and retain your progress. Technical diagnostics, where collected by the engine provider, may be used to maintain stability and troubleshoot problems. Analytics data collected through Firebase is used to understand which features players use and to improve the Game. We do not sell personal information. Service providers may process technical information under their own privacy terms where their technology is used in the Game.

6. Data retention and your choices

You can remove locally stored Game data through your device's app settings or by uninstalling the Game, subject to any operating-system backup behavior. For questions about diagnostic information or a privacy request, contact us using the email address below. We will review requests and direct you to the relevant service provider where necessary.

7. Children's privacy

The Game is not specifically directed to children. We do not knowingly request personal information from children through the Game. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so we can review the concern.

8. International processing

Third-party technology providers, including Unity and Google (Firebase) where applicable, may process technical information in countries other than your own, in accordance with their policies and applicable law.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the Game's features and services change. The revised version will be posted on this page with an updated date.
